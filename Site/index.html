The moment this is for

You are on the phone. One hand. Half a second.

Every other trainer is a study screen you read sitting down. The real moment is mid-call — the agent is waiting, you are three letters into a confirmation code and “S as in… Sierra?” is already too slow. Alpha Academy is built backwards from that moment: the chart is a primary surface, not a cheat sheet, and the drills use real strings instead of isolated letters.

How it trains

Four moves from “A as in… apple?” to fluent.

1

Learn the plate

Each letter arrives on a light card: the glyph, the code word, a respelled pronunciation key and one line on why the word is what it is. Tap to hear it spoken.

2

Drill both directions

Letter to word and word back to letter. Each symbol carries its own mastery level, and the app schedules what you are about to forget instead of what you already know.

3

Read a real string

Encode a booking reference or an email address in order, or listen to one being spelled and write down what you heard. This is the exercise the rest of the app exists to feed.

4

Keep it warm

A Daily Drill built from your weak letters plus one string, and an optional reminder at a time you choose. Two minutes a day holds the whole chart.
